Common questions

How do I tear off a page?
Draw a finger across the sheet, from either side, or lift it by a bottom corner. One page a day: tomorrow's is underneath.
Where do the pages I tore go?
Into History — the strip under the calendar, and the month grid behind the History tab. Tap any page to read it again. Tap the ribbon to keep a verse.
How do I change the reminder time?
The bell in the top-left corner. Reminders can also be turned off there entirely.
I paid, and the app still asks me to.
Open the paywall and use Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in with the one you bought it on.
How do I cancel a subscription?
iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DayRite. Cancelling stops the renewal; the days you have already paid for stay yours.
I lost my pages after reinstalling.
Pages live on the device only, so deleting the app deletes them. Your purchase is not lost — restore it as above.
Can I get it in another language?
Not yet. DayRite is in English today.
